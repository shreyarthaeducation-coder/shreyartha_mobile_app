## Full Description (4000 chars max)

```
Shreyartha Education is India's most comprehensive AI-powered educational platform for students, parents, and schools.

🎓 BUILT FOR STUDENTS (Class 6–12 & Beyond)
Whether you're preparing for board exams, JEE, NEET, or planning your career, Shreyartha gives you all the tools in one app.

📚 ACADEMIC IQ
Master every subject with our intelligent Academic IQ system. Get chapter-wise assessments, understand your strengths and gaps, and receive personalized recommendations to improve your scores.

🎯 CAREER ASSESSMENT & GUIDANCE
Confused about your future? Our career assessment helps you discover your ideal career path based on your strengths, interests, and aptitude. Get subject-specific guidance and university recommendations.

🌐 LANGUAGE LEARNING
Learn to communicate in 22+ Indian languages including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, Marathi, and more. Our AI-powered translation and text-to-speech helps you practice naturally.

🏆 COMPETITIVE EXAM PREPARATION
Comprehensive preparation for JEE, NEET, UPSC, and 50+ competitive exams. Practice with topic-wise mock tests, analyze your performance, and focus on weak areas.

🧠 PSYCHOMETRIC ASSESSMENT
Discover your learning style, personality traits, and cognitive strengths with our scientifically designed psychometric assessments. Understand how you think and learn best.

🤝 COUNSELLING & MENTORSHIP
Connect with expert counselors for academic guidance, career planning, and personal development. Book sessions, track progress, and get answers to your questions.

🛠️ SKILLS EDGE
Future-proof your skills with modules in communication, critical thinking, creativity, leadership, and emotional intelligence — skills that matter beyond the classroom.

🤖 CODING, AI & ROBOTICS
Introduction to programming, artificial intelligence, and robotics for the next generation of innovators.

📊 PROGRESS TRACKING
See your learning journey at a glance. Track attendance, completion rates, assessment scores, and academic improvement over time.

FOR PARENTS 👨‍👩‍👧
Monitor your child's academic progress, attendance, and learning activity from the parent portal.

FOR SCHOOLS 🏫
Teachers, counselors, and principals can manage academic content, track student wellness, and communicate through our dedicated school staff portal.

🔒 SAFE & PRIVATE
Your data is encrypted and never shared with third parties. Full account deletion available via the web portal or by contacting support@shreyartha.com. Read our Privacy Policy at shreyartha.com/privacy.

Join 10,000+ students, 500+ schools, and educators across India and 50+ countries already using Shreyartha.

Download now and unlock your potential.
```
